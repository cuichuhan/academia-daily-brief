Informing Entrepreneurs? Initial Public Offerings and New Business Formation

John M. Barrios (Yale University and NBER) · Jung Ho Choi (Stanford University) · Yael V. Hochberg (Rice University and NBER) · Jinhwan Kim (Stanford University) · Miao Liu (Boston College) · created 2026-08-17

方法
实证研究，结合EDGAR文件下载数据度量信息扩散，辅以503名创业者的实地问卷调查
数据
